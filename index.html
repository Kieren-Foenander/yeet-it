How to play
  • Everyone gets the same nine-letter word each day. It changes at midnight UTC.
  • Make a shorter word from your letters. Rearrange freely; use each letter as often as it appears.
  • Every word must drop at least one letter and keep at least three.
  • Unused letters are yeeted forever. Each new word scores 1; the starting word scores nothing. Best possible: 6.
  • Dropping several letters at once skips lengths, and skipped lengths are lost points.
  • Invalid words cost nothing. Submitted words can’t be undone.
  • The run ends at three letters, when no shorter word is left, or when you finish.
  • Your result shows lengths 8 down to 3: green for a word made, black for a length missed.
