The rules, quickly
Use only letters from your current word, as many times as they appear. Rearranging is allowed. Each new word must have fewer letters and at least three letters. You can drop multiple letters, but skipped word lengths cost you potential points. Each submitted word earns one point; the starting word earns none. Invalid attempts cost nothing. Submitted moves cannot be undone.
The run ends at three letters, when no shorter word is available, or when you choose to finish. Green share rows are word lengths you made. Black rows are lengths you missed. Reset and extra puzzles are available for this playtest.
