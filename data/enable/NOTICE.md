# ENABLE word list

`enable1.txt` is the ENABLE (Enhanced North American Benchmark LExicon) word list,
copied byte-for-byte from https://github.com/dolph/dictionary at commit
`694bf9529e986f9175dcbf5fe5875d111941b708`
(https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dolph/dictionary/694bf9529e986f9175dcbf5fe5875d111941b708/enable1.txt).

- SHA-256: `3f16130220645692ed49c7134e24a18504c2ca55b3c012f7290e3e77c63b1a89`
  (checked by `src/generator/sources.ts` before every generation or verification)
- 172,823 lines; 105,145 lowercase a–z entries of 3–9 letters are used by the game.
- ENABLE was released into the public domain by its authors (Alan Beale and
  M. Cooper, 1997–2000). The dolph/dictionary repository declares no licence of its
  own; the file is redistributed here as the public-domain ENABLE list.

ENABLE contains only lowercase a–z words, so it has no punctuation, spaces,
capitalised proper names, or abbreviations to strip. That is a property of the
source list, not an independent classification: some entries are rare, archaic,
regional, or borrowed, and they are all accepted in play. Plurals, verb forms, and
regional spellings in the list are accepted as-is.
